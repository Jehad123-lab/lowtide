The studio

Two people, a long table, and a view of the river.

Lowtide was started in 2024 by a designer and a developer who kept getting hired as a pair. We work from Dhaka and from wherever the client is, in small, focused seasons.

How we work

One conversation a week, one build you can open on your phone from day three, and no decks. We’d rather show you the thing.

Principles

  1. 01

    Pace over polish

    Timing is the brand. We tune easing before we tune colour.

  2. 02

    Respect the device

    Reduced-motion users get a still page. Phones get a lighter sim. Nobody gets a hot laptop.

  3. 03

    Borrow technique, not assets

    We study the best sites in the world and write our own code.
